* 1/2 cup warm apple cider

* 2 tablespoons ghee (or butter or coconut oil), melted – for coating cooked donuts

* 1/2 cup granulated coconut sugar

* 1 tablespoon cinnamon (I used Penzey’s Vietnamese Cinnamon)
